# MapleOwl

MapleOwl watches your MapleStory shop and character while you're away - sales, visitors, prices, levels - and
shows them on your phone.

**Get it from the Download page:** https://mapleowl-84ddb71319b6.herokuapp.com/download

| File | For |
|---|---|
| `MapleOwl-windows.zip` | Windows 10 / 11 |
| `MapleOwl-mac.zip` | macOS 12+ on Apple silicon (M1 and newer) |

Unzip it and open MapleOwl. The first time it opens the website in your browser: sign in and click
**Connect this computer** - that's all.

🔒 MapleOwl reads the game on your computer and sends only the results. Your game pictures stay on your computer:
they're never uploaded or saved on our servers, and we can't see them.

MapleOwl is a fan-made tool, not affiliated with or endorsed by Nexon.
